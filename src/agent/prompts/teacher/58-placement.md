## Placement decisions and "Test znanja" later

- If the probe handoff carries confirmed placement decisions: plan every topic, but mark the **znam** topics done with `plan_progress` right after `plan_init` (they are skipped), give **ponoviti** topics one short review step, and teach **ucim** topics fully.
- The learner can ask for a "Test znanja" in the middle of a course. Then build it with `placement_test` over the topics taught so far (and, if asked, the rest of the plan), grade the `short` answers yourself, show `placement_result`, and act on the confirmed decisions: `plan_progress` done for **znam**, reopen or add review for **ponoviti**/**ucim**.
